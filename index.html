Método básico · 8 steps

Beginner method

Layer by layer: white cross, white corners, middle layer, then the yellow side. The cube is held with yellow on top and white on the bottom. Each situation shows the cube as you'd see it at that step: pieces you haven't reached yet are grey. Pick one to watch it solved.

Método Fridrich · 119 formulas

CFOP overview

Cross, F2L, OLL, PLL. Where the beginner method needs about a minute, CFOP is the method behind sub-10 second solves. The full set is 41 F2L + 57 OLL + 21 PLL formulas.

1 · Cross

Solve the white cross on the bottom in one go, directly in its final position, with no daisy. Plan it during the 15 s inspection: a cross never needs more than 8 moves.

2 · F2L 41 cases

Pair each white corner with its middle-layer edge on top, then insert the pair in one formula. Four pairs finish the first two layers. Open F2L →

3 · OLL 57 cases

Make the whole top yellow in one formula. Start with Casos 01–07 (cross already done), then the rest. Open OLL →

4 · PLL 21 cases

Move the top pieces into place in one formula and finish the cube. Open PLL →

Learning order (from cubovelocidade)

  1. Practise the cross until you can do it directly in white, in one go, without pausing.
  2. Learn the first 7 OLL cases (cross already done), one per day, keeping the beginner method for the rest.
  3. Learn the 41 F2L cases, one or two per day or one block per week.
  4. Learn the 21 PLL cases, one per day or two per week, splitting long formulas into blocks.
  5. Finally, the remaining 50 OLL cases.

Expect to be slower at first. The site's author took about six months to learn every formula.

2-look OLL (10 formulas)

Look 1 · yellow cross. Line held horizontally: F R U R' U' F' (Caso 09). L held at the back-left: Fw R U R' U' Fw' (Caso 12). Dot: do one, then the other.

Look 2 · yellow corners. Casos 01–07, the seven cases with the cross already done.

2-look PLL (6 formulas)

Look 1 · corners. Headlights on one side: hold them at the back and do an A-perm. No headlights: Y-perm.

Look 2 · edges. U horário, U anti-horário, H or Z. The PLL page has a 2-look switch that animates this route for every case. Open PLL →

Practice habits

  • Use the timer with inspection on. Plan the full cross and try to track where the first F2L pair is.
  • Look ahead: turn slowly enough that you're finding the next pair while inserting the current one.
  • Mark cases as learned on the F2L, OLL and PLL pages to track your progress.
F2L · 41 cases

First two layers

Each case pairs a white corner with its edge and inserts them together. Thumbnails grey out the last layer so the pair stands out. Formulas that start with y or Dw re-grip the cube first, and the animation shows it.

OLL · 57 cases

Orient the last layer

One formula makes the whole top yellow. Diagrams show the top face with the back edge up; yellow stickers on the side strips face outward.

PLL · 21 cases

Permute the last layer

The top is already yellow; one formula moves the pieces home. Arrows show where pieces go. In 2-look mode each case is solved with corners first (A or Y), then edges (U, H or Z).

Movimentos

Notation

A letter turns that face clockwise as if you were looking straight at it. ' means anticlockwise, 2 means a half turn. Tap any move to watch it.

Sandbox

Write moves

Type any sequence and the cube performs it, continuing from where it is. Spaces are optional, so RUR'U' works. Wide moves can be written Rw or r.
